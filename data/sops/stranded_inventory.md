# Stranded Inventory FAQ

**Category code:** STR-04
**NRR Column R descriptive name:** Stranded Inventory FAQ
**Knowledge link GUID:** GD9QKLMN22DNC8ZZ

## When this applies
Units physically exist in a fulfillment center but the offer is inactive, so
the inventory cannot be sold. The inventory is "stranded" behind a listing
error, suppression, or a pricing/compliance block.

## Resolution steps
1. Identify the root listing error that stranded the inventory.
2. Guide the seller to fix the underlying listing issue (not just relist).
3. Confirm the offer reactivates and inventory becomes sellable.
4. If stranded > 30 days, flag for the removal/liquidation workflow.

## NRR audit guidance
- Column R descriptive name must read exactly: "Stranded Inventory FAQ".
- Attach knowledge link GUID GD9QKLMN22DNC8ZZ.
