# Customer Reviews FAQ

**Category code:** REV-02
**NRR Column R descriptive name:** Customer Reviews FAQ
**Knowledge link GUID:** GDTT77XCRV00NC81

## When this applies
A customer product review is reported as policy-violating (abusive, fake,
incentivized, or containing prohibited content).

## Resolution steps
1. Do NOT action the review directly within LCI.
2. Route the case to the Community / Reviews team who own review moderation.
3. Record the handoff and the Community case reference.

## NRR audit guidance
- Column R descriptive name must read exactly: "Customer Reviews FAQ".
- Attach knowledge link GUID GDTT77XCRV00NC81.
- Any LCI agent actioning a review directly is a process miss — flag it.
